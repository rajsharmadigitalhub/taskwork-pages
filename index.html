About TaskWork

TaskWork is a productivity app for managing tasks, habits, events, alarms, shopping lists, notes, focus activities, and other personal productivity information.

TaskWork can use Google Sign-In and Google Drive when a user chooses to back up or restore supported app data.

Privacy Policy

TaskWork stores app information on the user's device. When the user chooses Google Drive backup, TaskWork requests permission to access Google Drive for the purpose of creating, updating, and restoring TaskWork backup data.

TaskWork does not sell users' Google account information. Google Drive access is used for the backup and restore functionality requested by the user.

Users can manage or revoke TaskWork's Google account access through their Google Account settings.

Terms of Service

TaskWork is provided as a productivity application. Users are responsible for maintaining access to their devices and Google accounts and for reviewing their backup data.

Features may be changed, improved, or updated over time. Use of Google services is also subject to Google's applicable terms and policies.
